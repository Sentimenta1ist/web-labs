Hello, I am Steve Jobs, An American business magnate, industrial designer, investor, and media proprietor. A pioneer of the personal computer revolution of the 1970s and 1980s, along with Apple co-founder Steve Wozniak.







Facts about myself

Born

Stephen Jobs was born in San Francisco in 1955, where he was adopted by Paul and Clara Job. They named him Stephen Paul. Jobs's biological parents are Syrian Abdulfattah Jandali and American Joan Simpson.

Pixar is mine.

In 1986, Jobs bought The Graphics Group, now Pixar, for $ 5 million. Following Disney's purchase of Pixar, Jobs became the largest private shareholder in The Walt Disney Company with a 7% stake.

Bye, Apple

On August 24, 2011, Jobs announced his resignation as Apple CEO.
